## 2. Description of Service

Mini Weather is a privacy-first mobile application for iOS that delivers real-time weather forecasts, Air Quality Index (AQI) reports, day-over-day temperature comparisons, interactive visual widgets, and localized atmospheric metrics.

Weather and environmental data displayed in the App are sourced ephemerally from third-party APIs, including **Apple WeatherKit** and **WeatherAPI.com**.

## 5. Location Services & Device Permissions

- Mini Weather utilizes optional device Location Services to retrieve localized weather conditions for your current coordinate position.
- Location access can be granted, restricted, or revoked at any time via:  
  `iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mini Weather`
- If location permissions are denied, the App functions fully by allowing you to manually search and select any city worldwide.
