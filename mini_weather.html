Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy sets forth the principles and practices governing the processing of technical data by Mini Weather ("the Application"). Distributed via the Apple App Store, the Application is designed to strictly satisfy Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1.1 — Data Collection and Storage) and accurately corresponds to the App Privacy Details disclosed on the App Store product page.

2. Purpose & Processing Operations

2.1 Location Data Processing

The Application requests temporary location permission solely to determine geographical coordinates for local weather retrieval. Coordinates are transmitted via encrypted TLS (HTTPS) to:

  • Apple WeatherKit — Primary meteorological data service; and
  • WeatherAPI.com — Air Quality Index (AQI) data service and secondary fallback source.

Location data is never stored off-device, never combined with ad networks, and never sold. Users may grant, modify, or revoke location access at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mini Weather. If access is revoked, the Application permits manual city search and selection.

2.2 Geocoding Queries

When searching for a city, the query string is transmitted securely to Apple's CLGeocoder API solely to resolve coordinate bounds. Search strings are not logged or stored.

2.3 Local Device Cache

The Application maintains local key-value storage on your iOS device for:

  • Selected city names and recent search queries;
  • Cached weather data and day-over-day temperature snapshots;
  • User preferences (temperature unit, distance unit, language, font style, forecast range, glass opacity, notification settings); and
  • Widget payload stored inside the shared App Group container (group.nothing_weather) for iOS Home and Lock Screen widgets.

2.4 Local Notifications

The optional daily briefing feature schedules notifications locally on your device. The Application does not utilize remote push servers, and no notification activity is reported off-device.

4. Data Retention & Erasure

  • Zero Remote Retention: Because no central servers are maintained by the Developer, no remote user records exist to retain or delete.
  • User-Initiated Deletion: You may permanently purge all application cache and settings by uninstalling the Application or navigating to iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mini Weather → Delete App.
  • Account Deletion Standard: As no user accounts exist, Apple's in-app account deletion requirement (Guideline 5.1.1(v)) does not apply.

5. External References & Resources

The Application may contain hyperlinks to external sites (such as legal documentation or attribution resources). The Developer is not responsible for the privacy standards of external websites. Users are advised to inspect the policies of third-party domains upon visiting.

6. Protection of Minors (COPPA & EEA Standards)

Mini Weather is rated 4+ and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 within the European Economic Area). In accordance with COPPA and global child protection laws, because no personal information is gathered, no minor data is stored or distributed.

7. Statutory Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD)

Under applicable international privacy mandates — including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) — users possess statutory rights concerning their data:

  • Right of Access & Inspection: Review all cached preferences directly within the Application's Settings panel.
  • Right to Erasure: Erase all local state at any time by removing the Application.
  • Right to Revoke Permissions: Manage location and notification authorizations within iOS System Settings.
  • Non-Sale Guarantee: The Developer does not sell, rent, or share personal data under any circumstances.

8. Technical & Architectural Security

The Application enforces robust technical safeguards:

  • Sandboxed Isolation: Local configuration data resides within iOS's encrypted-at-rest application sandbox.
  • TLS Transmission: 100% of network traffic uses Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Explicit Authorizations: Location access is requested in-context and restricted to active usage ("While Using").
