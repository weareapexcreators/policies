Continuous Docking, Screen Wakelock & OLED Safety

Lumina is engineered as an active landscape desk and nightstand display:

  • Screen Wakelock: The app utilizes system wakelock controls (`wakelock_plus`) to keep your screen illuminated while docked. Prolonged screen use naturally consumes battery; we recommend connecting your device to an Apple/OEM-certified charger during continuous docking.
  • OLED Burn-In Shield: Modern AMOLED and OLED displays are susceptible to static pixel fatigue. Lumina includes an automated Burn-In Protection feature that subtly shifts clock coordinates by 1–2 pixels every 60 seconds. While designed to reduce retention risk, Abhinav Kumar and Apex Creators cannot warrant against inherent manufacturer hardware wear, panel fatigue, or external charger thermal stress.
  • Thermal Management: Users are responsible for ensuring adequate device airflow while charging overnight or running high-refresh-rate displays. Discontinue continuous charging if your device becomes excessively hot.
  • Productivity Tools: Integrated timers and stopwatches are productivity companions and should not be used as the sole timing device for life-critical medical, safety, or legal deadlines.
