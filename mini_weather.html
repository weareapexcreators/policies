Privacy Policy

Mini Weather • Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Privacy Summary: Mini Weather is a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, track, or share your personal data. All processing occurs locally on your device.

1. Information Handling & Data Collection

We do not operate external servers and do not collect or store personal information. Under App Store Privacy declarations, Mini Weather is classified as "No Data Collected".

Data Category Collected Off-Device Processed On-Device Purpose
Location Data No Yes (Ephemeral) Fetch local weather and air quality conditions
Search Queries No Yes (Ephemeral) Resolve searched cities via geocoding API
App Preferences No Yes (Local Cache) Save local settings and widget preferences
Analytics & Tracking No No Zero ad frameworks or analytics SDKs

2. Location & Data Usage

Location permissions are used solely to fetch weather conditions for your current coordinates. Your location data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections to:

  • Apple WeatherKit — Weather data provider
  • WeatherAPI.com — Air Quality Index (AQI) and fallback provider

Your coordinates are never stored, tracked, or sold. Location access can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mini Weather.

3. Local Storage

All app settings, temperature unit preferences, cached weather reports, and widget states are stored strictly within your device's sandboxed storage (`group.nothing_weather`). None of this data is transmitted off-device.

4. Third-Party Services

The app communicates only with necessary service endpoints over secure HTTPS:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • WeatherAPI.com Privacy Policy

5. Data Control & Rights

You have total control over your data. Deleting the application or clearing app storage in iOS Settings permanently removes all cached data from your device.
