Privacy Policy for LocalAI: Offline AI Chat LLM

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

================================================================================

1. Introduction

Welcome to LocalAI: Offline AI Chat LLM ("the App"). The App is developed
and operated by Abhinav Kumar, operating as Apex Creators ("we," "our," or
"us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have
complete control over your data.

LocalAI is built from the ground up as a 100% ad-free, privacy-first, on-device
artificial intelligence engine. All inference, document parsing, chat history,
and voice processing occur locally on your hardware. We do NOT serve ads, do
NOT sell your data, do NOT run tracking scripts, and do NOT maintain cloud servers
to collect your conversations.

This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices in compliance with
the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (specifically Guideline 5.1.1 Data
Collection & Storage), Google Play Store Developer Policies, General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and Children's
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as of August 2026.

2. Data Collection and Usage Practices

2.1 Zero Personal Data Collection
We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or any
personal identity information to use the App.

2.2 100% On-Device Local Chat & Files
- Chat Conversations: Your conversation history, prompts, system instructions,
  and AI responses are stored exclusively in an encrypted, local SQLite database
  on your device. We NEVER collect, transmit, or monitor your chat logs.
- Document & Image Attachments: When you use document parsing (PDFs, DOCX, XLSX,
  CSV, TXT) or in-app vision features, all files and camera captures are processed
  entirely on your device hardware by on-device LLM models. Files are never uploaded
  to our or any third-party servers.
- Voice & Speech Processing: Text-to-Speech (TTS) and Speech-to-Text (STT) run
  locally using your device's native engines. Audio is processed on-device and
  is never recorded or transmitted remotely by us.

2.3 Complete Absence of Advertisements and Tracking
The App is 100% AD-FREE. We do not use Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience
Network, or any other third-party advertising SDKs.
- No Advertising Identifiers: We do not access, collect, or store device Advertising
  IDs (Apple IDFA / Android Advertising ID).
- No App Tracking: The App does not track you across third-party websites or apps.
  Under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and Google's Play Data
  Safety guidelines, LocalAI collects zero tracking data.

3. External Network Connections & Third-Party Privacy Policies

LocalAI operates completely offline once models are downloaded. Internet access
is used strictly for optional features and third-party open-source downloads.
Below are the third-party services that may be accessed, along with direct links
to their respective privacy policies:

3.1 Open-Source Model Downloads (Hugging Face)
When you browse or download open-source GGUF AI models in the Models Hub, your
device connects directly via HTTPS to Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Standard
HTTP network requests (including your IP address) are processed directly by
Hugging Face in accordance with their privacy policy:
- Hugging Face Privacy Policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy
We do not track, log, or record which models you download.

3.2 Open-Source Fonts & Static Resources (Google Fonts)
The App may utilize open-source font families (such as Inter, Roboto, or Outfit)
provided via Google Fonts. Static typography assets or API font references are
governed by Google's Privacy Policy:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple App Store / Google Play)
If you choose to support development by purchasing an optional subscription or
in-app item:
- Financial transactions, billing details, and receipt validation are processed
  directly and securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store).
- LocalAI does NOT receive, process, or store your credit card numbers or payment credentials.
- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 Optional Cloud Providers (Bring Your Own Key - BYOK)
If you explicitly choose to configure third-party Cloud Providers (such as OpenAI,
Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, or Mistral AI) in Settings:
- Your API keys are encrypted and stored solely on your device in hardware-backed secure storage.
- Session prompts and messages are transmitted directly from your device to the selected
  provider's official API endpoint via TLS/HTTPS.
- We do not proxy, inspect, intercept, or store any data sent to third-party cloud providers.
- Data sent to third-party providers is governed by their respective privacy policies:
  * OpenAI Privacy Policy:       https://openai.com/privacy/
  * Anthropic Privacy Policy:    https://privacy.anthropic.com/
  * OpenRouter Privacy Policy:   https://openrouter.ai/privacy
  * Google Gemini Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  * Groq Privacy Policy:         https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
  * Mistral AI Privacy Policy:   https://mistral.ai/terms/#privacy-policy

4. Local Device Permissions

LocalAI requests only essential device permissions to enable local features. All
data accessed through these permissions remains strictly on your local device:

- Storage / Media Permission: Used to save downloaded GGUF AI models locally, to
  read documents attached by you, and to store local chat database backups.
- Camera Permission: Used strictly if you choose to capture a photo using the
  in-app camera to process image inputs with on-device vision models.
- Microphone Permission: Used strictly if you enable voice mode for hands-free local
  speech recognition.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

LocalAI does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children
under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). Because all processing is local and no
personal data is gathered, no children's data is stored or shared.

Because AI models generate responses dynamically based on user prompts, we advise
that minors under 18 use the App under parental or legal guardian supervision.

7. User Rights & Complete Data Erasure (Apple Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) & GDPR/CCPA)

Under global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, and Apple
Developer Guidelines):
- Right to Access & Export: You can view, copy, or export your full chat history
  at any time directly within the App.
- Right to Immediate Erasure / Deletion: You have total control over all local data.
  * Individual Session Deletion: Swipe or tap delete on any chat session to permanently remove it.
  * Clear All Data: Go to App Settings -> Clear All Data to immediately purge all stored chat logs,
    saved documents, and hardware-stored API keys.
  * Model Deletion: Delete downloaded GGUF models anytime from the Models Hub.
  * App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App instantly and permanently destroys all local SQLite
    databases, cached models, and secure keychain key entries.
- Do Not Sell or Share Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or share personal
  information with third parties under any circumstances.

8. Security & Hardware Protection

Your privacy and security are safeguarded by our local-first architecture:
- No central database or cloud storage of chat logs to breach.
- API keys for cloud providers are stored in hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- All network communications for model downloads or optional cloud APIs are encrypted
  using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS).
