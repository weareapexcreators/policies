1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Sticker & Photo Widget: Theme (internally referenced as Stickr). The application is developed and operated by Abhinav Kumar, operating as Apex Creators. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your home screen customization data.

The App is engineered from the ground up on a local-first, on-device architecture. Your personal gallery photos, custom widget styles, and layout configurations are stored and processed exclusively on your device. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not monitor your device usage, and we do not sell or monetize personal user data.

This document outlines our data handling practices in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as of September 2026.

2. Data Collection & Privacy Practices

2.1 Zero Personal Identity Collection

You can use the App without creating an account or providing an email address, phone number, name, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).

2.2 100% On-Device Photo Widgets & Custom Packs

  • Personal Gallery Photos: When you select a photo from your device gallery to place inside a home screen widget or to create a custom sticker pack, the image is processed and cached exclusively on your local hardware storage using standard Android storage isolation (Room SQLite & internal cache). Your personal photos are never transmitted, uploaded, or analyzed on remote servers.
  • Widget Configurations: All widget state settings (frame style, opacity, corner radius, rotation frequency, and background tints) remain solely inside the app's local Android Room database.
  • Favorite Stickers: Your marked favorite stickers and custom packs are saved locally in private device storage.

2.3 Complete Absence of Advertising & Ad Trackers

100% Ad-Free Experience

The App contains zero third-party advertisements. We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, or any advertising SDKs. We do not access, store, or transmit your Android Advertising ID (GAID).

3. Device Permissions & Usage

The App requests only the minimum required Android permissions necessary to provide personalization and widget features:

Permission Technical Scope Purpose
Photos & Media Access READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Android Photo Picker Allows you to choose photos from your gallery for home screen photo widgets and custom packs. Never uploaded externally.
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Required exclusively to download sticker packs and process subscription verification via Google Play Billing.
Widget Updates ACTION_APPWIDGET_UPDATE Used by the Android launcher system to render and refresh responsive Jetpack Glance widgets on your home screen.

5. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases

The App offers optional digital products to unlock unlimited downloads, exclusive frames (Polaroid, Dark Surface), and VIP access:

  • Monthly Pro Pass: Auto-renewing subscription charged monthly via your Google Play Account.
  • Annual Pro Pass: Auto-renewing subscription charged annually with discounted pricing.
  • Lifetime VIP Pass: Non-consumable, one-time payment granting perpetual access to all current and future packs.

Cancellation & Management: Subscriptions can be managed or canceled at any time directly in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Canceling a subscription retains your Pro benefits until the end of the current billing cycle.

6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety declaration requirements:

  • Data Collected: No personal identification data (name, email, phone, location) is collected or stored by Apex Creators.
  • Data Shared: Zero personal data is shared with third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • Data Encryption in Transit: All remote network communications (catalog queries, sticker downloads, billing requests) use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Data Deletion Mechanism: All user-created widget data, favorites, and downloaded stickers are stored locally on the device and can be permanently deleted by the user at any time.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Regulations)

The App is designed for general audiences and personalization enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the application operates locally without account registration or remote user profiles, no personal data from minors is harvested, retained, or disclosed.

8. User Rights & Complete Data Erasure (GDPR / CCPA)

Under global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the UK GDPR, you have full ownership and rights over your data:

  • Right to Access & Control: All your custom widget configurations and sticker packs are directly viewable and manageable inside the App.
  • Right to Immediate Deletion:
    • You can delete any custom pack, sticker, or widget configuration directly inside the App with immediate permanent effect.
    • You can clear all cached sticker assets and preferences anytime via your Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Stickr > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Cache).
    • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all Room SQLite database records, DataStore preferences, and downloaded sticker files from your device.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Therefore, no opt-out request is required.

9. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We enforce strict security safeguards to protect your device data:

  • Local Sandbox Isolation: Android application sandboxing prevents other applications from inspecting your personal widget photos or local Room database files.
  • Zero Remote Storage: Because your personal photos and widget compositions are never stored on external servers, there is zero risk of remote database breaches or cloud leaks.
  • Transport Layer Security: All sticker catalog synchronization and Google Play billing transactions occur exclusively over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
