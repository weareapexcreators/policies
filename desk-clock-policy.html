Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

We believe in strict data minimization. Lumina is designed as an ambient utility, not a data-harvesting network.

A. Information We DO NOT Collect:

  • We do not require you to register an account, sign in, or provide your real name, email address, phone number, or home address.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photo gallery, or SMS logs.
  • We do not run background behavioral profiling or cross-app analytics tracking.
  • We have completely removed Firebase Analytics and third-party tracking telemetry from our application binary.

B. Information Processed Locally on Your Device:

  • Preferences & Settings: Selected watch face, color palette customizations, 12/24-hour time format, temperature scale (°C/°F), and keep-screen-on toggles are stored locally via SharedPreferences (Android) and NSUserDefaults (iOS).
  • Stopwatch & Timer Laps: Local lap counts and timer states are saved in a local SQLite sandbox database. This data never leaves your device.

C. Information Processed via Third-Party SDKs:

When accessing network features (such as real-time weather forecasts or viewing advertisements in the free tier), authorized third-party SDKs process standard network metadata (such as IP address, device type, operating system version, and anonymous advertising identifiers) in compliance with international privacy laws.

07

Continuous Docking, Screen Wakelock & Hardware Safety

Lumina is engineered as a dedicated StandBy desk and nightstand clock:

  • Screen Wakelock: The app utilizes the system screen wakelock permission (`wakelock_plus`) to prevent your display from turning off while docked. We recommend connecting your device to a certified charger during prolonged use.
  • OLED Burn-In Shield: Modern AMOLED and OLED displays can suffer static pixel fatigue. Lumina incorporates an automated pixel micro-shifting algorithm that moves clock coordinates by 1–2 pixels every 60 seconds to protect hardware integrity.
  • Productivity Tools Disclaimer: The integrated stopwatch and Pomodoro timer are personal productivity companions and should not be used as the sole timing device for life-critical medical, safety, or legal deadlines.
