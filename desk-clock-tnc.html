Continuous Docking, Screen Wakelock & Device Safety

Lumina is engineered as a dedicated landscape desk clock and bedside StandBy monitor. By using the Application, you acknowledge and agree to the following operational parameters:

  • Persistent Screen WakeLock: The Application utilizes system wakelock flags to keep your device screen illuminated continuously while docked. Continuous display operation consumes battery power; we recommend connecting your device to a certified charger during long-term docking.
  • OLED / AMOLED Burn-In Shield: Modern OLED and AMOLED panels are susceptible to static pixel fatigue over long durations. Lumina includes an automated Burn-In Protection feature that subtly shifts clock coordinates every 60 seconds. While engineered to mitigate retention risk, Apex Creators is not liable for inherent manufacturer hardware degradation, panel wear, or thermal stress caused by external chargers or prolonged ambient display use.
  • Thermal Management: Users are responsible for ensuring adequate device ventilation while charging and running high-refresh-rate displays. If your device becomes hot, remove heavy insulating cases and disconnect non-certified rapid chargers.
  • Timer & Stopwatch Suite: The built-in visual timers and precision stopwatches are productivity companions. They should not be relied upon as the exclusive timing mechanism for medical prescriptions, life-support operations, or high-stakes examination cutoffs.
