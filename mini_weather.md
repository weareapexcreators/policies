*All data processed on-device is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you in any way, per store guidelines.*

## 2. How Data Is Used

### 2.1 Location

The App uses your device's location only to request weather for your current position. Your coordinates are sent over HTTPS to:

- **Apple WeatherKit** — primary weather data provider; and
- **WeatherAPI.com** — air quality data and fallback weather source.

Your location is never used to track you, is never combined with advertising, and is never sold. You can revoke or change location permission at any time via *iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Mini Weather*. If you deny location access, the App falls back to a location you select manually by city name.

### 2.2 Search

When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple's geocoding service solely to resolve the place you are looking for. Search queries are not saved by the App.

### 2.3 Local Device Storage

The App stores the following data on your device only:

- Your manually selected location and recent search selections
- Cached weather data (including a temperature snapshot used for the day-over-day comparison)
- Preferences (temperature unit, distance unit, language, font style, forecast range, glass opacity, notification setting)

Widget data is stored in the App's shared App Group container (`group.nothing_weather`) so the iOS Home and Lock Screen widgets can display it. **None of this locally stored data is transmitted anywhere.**

### 2.4 Notifications

The optional daily morning briefing is scheduled locally on your device. The App does not use push notifications, and no notification data is collected.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

- The developer does not operate servers and therefore does not retain any of your data.
- All App data lives locally on your device.
- You can delete all data at any time by **uninstalling the App**, or by resetting the App's data via *iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mini Weather*.
- The App does not support account creation, so no in-app account-deletion flow is required (Apple's account-deletion requirement does not apply).

## 7. User Rights & Data Control (GDPR / CCPA / Global Laws)

Under global privacy regulations — including the GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, LGPD, and PIPEDA — you have the following rights:

- **Right to Access & Export** — You can view and review all App data and preferences at any time directly within the App's Settings screen.
- **Right to Erasure / Deletion** — You can clear all application data at any time from the App Settings or via iOS System Settings (*Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mini Weather*). Since all data resides locally on your device, clearing app storage permanently and irreversibly deletes all your data.
- **Right to Revoke Permissions** — You can revoke location permission (*iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services*) and disable notifications (the App's Settings or *iOS Settings → Notifications*) at any time.
- **Do Not Sell or Share Personal Information** — The developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties under any circumstances.
- **Contact** — Email the developer at any time with privacy questions, requests, or complaints. GDPR Art. 15–21 and California CCPA/CPRA requests can be sent to the contact below; because no data is collected by the developer, such requests are resolved by the controls listed above.

## 8. Security

Privacy and security are safeguarded by the App's local-first architecture:

- No central database or cloud storage of weather data to breach.
- All App data is stored in iOS's sandboxed, encrypted-on-device storage.
- All network communications to weather and geocoding providers are encrypted using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS).
- Permissions (location, notifications) are requested in-context, and location access can be limited to "While Using" the App.
